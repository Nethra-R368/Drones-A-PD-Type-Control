Control Methodology Overview

Finite-Time PPF (FTPPF)

$$\rho(t) = \begin{cases} (\rho_0 - \rho_T(1 + t/T_s))\exp(\kappa t/(t - T_s)) + \rho_T, & 0 \le t < T_s \\ \rho_T, & t \ge T_s \end{cases}$$ This defines a predefined performance envelope that converges precisely in finite time $T_s$.

Constrained PD Controller

$$u = -K_p q_v - K_d \omega - u_{cons}$$ $$u_{cons} = \left[ \alpha(I - E_1)^{-2} + \beta(I - E_2)^{-2} \right] s + \hat{\theta}\text{sat}(s/\varsigma)$$ As states approach their boundaries, $E_1, E_2 \to I$, causing the barrier term to grow and steer the spacecraft back inside the envelope.

Adaptive Disturbance Estimation

$$\dot{\hat{\theta}} = \frac{1}{\eta_1} \left( \|s\| - \eta_2 \hat{\theta} \right)$$ $$\eta_2 = 2 \exp(-0.5t)$$ Compensates for time-varying disturbances $d(t)$ and actuator saturation effects without prior knowledge of their bounds.
